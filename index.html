Build notes

Three things I built (and what I skipped)

This very site

Built blank-folder-to-live in an afternoon: static HTML, a shared canvas "share-card" engine, and a pastel watercolour brand in one CSS file. No framework, no database.

Skipped: a CMS. Every page is just files — easy to host anywhere, free.

The share-card pattern

Every tool exports a 1080×1920 PNG from a single reusable canvas function. Build it once, reuse it everywhere — the card is the marketing.

Skipped: server-side image rendering. The browser draws it; nothing leaves your device.

Privacy by default

Life in Weeks never sends your birthday anywhere. Aligned encodes its quiz state in the URL, so two people can play with zero backend.

Skipped: accounts and tracking, until a tool genuinely needs them.

About

From notes to tiny machines

I spend my days deep in content and data — competitive intelligence for education YouTube, transcripts, market data. Along the way I kept building little tools to make the work less manual.

This shed is where the general-audience ones live: small, fast, and built so the output is something you'd actually want to post. I'm an operator who learned to ship with AI in the terminal — the judgment of what to build matters more than the code.
